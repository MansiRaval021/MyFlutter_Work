Zomato – Saved Addresses & Cart
Local storage can keep saved addresses and cart items on the device, so users can still view or manage them when the internet is temporarily unavailable.

WhatsApp – Chat Messages
WhatsApp stores messages locally on the phone, allowing users to read previously loaded chats even without an internet connection.

Spotify – Downloaded Music
Spotify stores downloaded songs locally, allowing users to listen to their playlists offline without an active internet connection.
